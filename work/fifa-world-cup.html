FIFA WORLD CUP
HALFTIME SHOW
ART DIRECTION 
 MOTION

Motion direction for the FIFA World Cup Final Halftime Show at MetLife Stadium, developing the visual concepts and leading the DE-YAN motion team to bring each artist’s performance to life.

A cohesive visual language connected the artists’ distinct worlds through expressive typography, bold color, and a handcrafted aesthetic, bringing warmth and personality to the stadium’s monumental scale. From early visual studies through animation and on-site refinement, the work shaped the rhythm and storytelling of the live show and its global broadcast.


CLIENT: DONE & DUSTED / AGENCY: DE-YAN
