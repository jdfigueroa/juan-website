BRANDCAST 2026ART DIRECTIONMOTION

YouTube Brandcast 2026 brought live performances by Chappell Roan and Zara Larsson to Lincoln Center’s David Geffen Hall, with art direction and visual content tailored to each artist’s world.

Chappell Roan’s presentation created a magical extension of the physical stage into the screen, blending scenic elements and digital imagery into a continuous environment. For Zara Larsson’s performance, custom 3D animated plants expanded her visual universe, creating a distinct botanical world for each song.

Creative Director for Chappell Roan’s performance: Griffin Stoddard.
