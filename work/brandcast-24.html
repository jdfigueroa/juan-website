BRANDCAST 24ART DIRECTIONMOTION

Art direction for YouTube Brandcast 2024, the platform’s annual upfront presentation, held at David Geffen Hall at Lincoln Center in New York City. The work included visual direction for Billie Eilish’s closing performance and leading motion development for K-pop group Stray Kids’ presentation. Bringing together global music artists, creators, and industry leaders, the event celebrated YouTube’s influence on contemporary entertainment and culture. The visual approach translated each artist’s distinct identity into a large-scale live experience, connecting motion, music, and stage design.


CLIENT: YOUTUBE / AGENCY: DE-YAN
