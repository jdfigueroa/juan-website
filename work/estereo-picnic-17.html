ART DIRECTION 
 CREATIVE DIRECTION

Each year, the artistic and audiovisual experience of the festival becomes stronger. This year, we paid tribute to the religion of our days: music. The sacred symbol is a missing cassette. Without the cassette, there is no party, no fun. Appearing in an alien spaceship, our new friends manage to save the party.  An intergalactic cassette, a teenage party, and a tribute to David Bowie. With several design awards, this is the most successful advertising campaign in the history of the renowned Colombian festival. The video's art was all handcrafted, evoking the nostalgia of the cassette generation.
