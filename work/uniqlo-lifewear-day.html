LIFEWEAR DAYART DIRECTIONMOTION DIRECTION

UNIQLO LifeWear Day 2025 brought “The Art and Science of LifeWear” to New York’s Museum of Modern Art, celebrating the brand’s 20th anniversary in the United States. Through interactive installations, material displays, and digital storytelling, the event translated LifeWear’s philosophy into a spatial experience exploring the intersection of art, technology, and everyday life. Across MoMA’s lobby and garden, installations explored five core principles: Design, Technology, Art, Sport, and Heart for All.

The experience highlighted UNIQLO’s textile innovations with Toray alongside its ongoing partnership with MoMA, connecting the science behind the clothing with a shared commitment to making art accessible. A live conversation featuring Clare Waight Keller, Roger Federer, and Cate Blanchett extended these themes, while the evening introduced KAWS as UNIQLO’s first Artist in Residence.
