CDM: MEMORIAART DIRECTIONIDENTITY

Logo design and art direction for Memoria, a collaboration between Colombian Drone Mafia and Mexican composer and violinist Gibrana Cervantes. Colombian Drone Mafia is the alias of Nyksan, a DJ and sound artist from Bogotá and one of the founders of TraTraTrax. The record brings together synthesis, field recordings, and layered strings to create a dark, immersive soundscape suspended between raw textures and delicate melodies.

A fragile geography connecting the Americas, where Colombian Drone Mafia and Gibrana Cervantes meet through sound, landscape, and memory.

Mountain and noise. Imbalance and harmony.
It is a lake in the sky, a forest between seas.
A still cloud in la cordillera, a name that floats.
