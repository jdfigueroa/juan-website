RUBIO: CALLA
MOTION
EDITING

RUBIO blends pop, hip-hop, and electronic beats with a hint of ethnic textures, creating a dream-like soundscape. Emerging from Chile’s underground scene, her music pushes boundaries and channels raw emotion with style.

The visualizer drifts through a hazy dream that warps and fades when you try to remember it. It moves through dark, winding mountain roads, a reflection of the emotional journey that comes with letting go of someone who’s no longer there.
