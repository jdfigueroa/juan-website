LOS ANGELES. US
(2022)

AMEX x SAKS
ART DIRECTION 
 MOTION

Art and motion direction for an immersive experience presented by American Express Platinum and Saks Fifth Avenue, featuring an exclusive live performance by Kacey Musgraves at The Lot at Formosa in LA.

The experience featured a 220-foot interactive projection that responded to guests’ movements, transforming the space into a dynamic digital environment. Monumental LED columns displayed custom audio-responsive visuals, creating an evolving visual landscape synchronized with the music.


AGENCY: DE-YAN / CLIENT: AMEX & SAKS
