CULTURA PROFÉTICA
ART DIRECTION
MOTION

Art direction for Cultura Profética’s first-ever vinyl release, Sobrevolando. An ode to social, political, and cultural awakening, the band’s sixth studio album won the Latin Grammy for Best Alternative Music Album and was released in a double-sleeve vinyl format.

Alongside this work, art direction for the band’s 2019 concert tour included a series of custom thirty-second animations for each song, created to accompany their live performances.


CLIENT: CULTURA PROFÉTICA

Concept: Willy Rodríguez, Soraya Abdouni , Juan David Figueroa / Illustration: Daniel Clarke & La Quebranta / Photography: Melissa Isabel Quiñones
