SACRIFICIO
ART DIRECTION 
 INTERACTIVE

Art direction for SACRIFICIO, the debut EP by Bogotá-born, London-based producer Nyksan, and contributing art direction for its accompanying interactive digital world.

The record explores dark, rhythmic ambient music, while the immersive environment brings together music, sculpture, digital art, and photography. Inspired by the landscapes of Chiribiquete (described as the “Sistine Chapel” of the archeology in America), this virtual world extends the EP’s sonic atmosphere into a space for exploration.


TECHNICAL DIRECTOR: CARLOS SERRANO / MUSIC: NICOLÁS SÁNCHEZ
