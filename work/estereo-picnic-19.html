BOGOTÁ. CO
(2018)

ESTÉREO PICNIC 19
ART DIRECTION
MOTION

Commissioned video for Festival Estéreo Picnic 2019 line-up release and art direction for the festival’s 10th anniversary. The video is a journey through the history of “The X”. The X stands for ten in Roman numbers. The X is a magic symbol that has existed since the beginning of our time.

Festival Estéreo Picnic celebrated its 10th edition on April 2019, in Bogotá, bringing together some of the biggest musical performances ever seen in Colombia with its most ambitious lineup to date.

The festival featured Arctic Monkeys, Kendrick Lamar, Twenty One Pilots and Sam Smith among many others. Together with an extensive lineup of international and local artists, the anniversary edition celebrated the musical diversity that has defined the festival since its beginnings.
